What the Oracle does

1 · Scores every stockEach week, after Friday's close, it ranks all S&P 500 stocks on 8 well-studied price signals using three models (two machine-learning models and a simple blend).
2 · Picks 10It suggests the top 10. A stock already held is kept while it stays in the top 50, which cuts trading costs.
3 · Locks the forecastThe full list is sealed with a fingerprint before the market opens, so it cannot be changed afterwards.
4 · Grades itselfAfter 5 trading days it scores the forecast against what really happened, and keeps a public track record.

Honest limits

  • Uses prices only. No earnings dates, news or company fundamentals yet, so earnings surprises can swamp the signal.
  • The edge is small and not yet statistically proven. Long losing streaks are normal.
  • Prices come from a free, unofficial source. Stocks that left the index are only partly covered, which can flatter history.
  • Research output, not financial advice. Paper-trade first.
