What the Oracle does

1 · Scores every stockWhen the previous list ends, at that day's close, it ranks all S&P 500 stocks on well-studied signals from several data sources (listed below) using three models (two machine-learning models and a simple blend).
2 · Picks 10It suggests the top 10. A stock already held is kept while it stays in the top 50, which cuts trading costs.
3 · Locks the forecastBefore the market opens, the full list is fingerprinted (SHA-256) and committed to the public Git history, so any later edit would be detectable.
4 · Grades itselfAfter 5 trading days it scores the forecast against what really happened, and keeps a public track record.

Honest limits

  • Model inputs: price signals. Company financials, filings, attention and macro data are collected and shown, and are tested before they are allowed into the model.
  • The edge is small and not yet statistically proven. Long losing streaks are normal.
  • Prices come from a free, unofficial source. Stocks that left the index are only partly covered, which can flatter history.
  • Research output, not financial advice. Paper-trade first.
